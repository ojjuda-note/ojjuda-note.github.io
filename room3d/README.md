# World room renderer

The supplied `ojjuda-3d-package-v17.zip` provides the furniture, pet and avatar models and embedded artwork. `engine.js` adapts those models to the existing World room data. The house stays large in the viewport. Its surroundings are a separate painted background, not extra 3D yard geometry included in the camera fit.

`world-room.js` mounts this same-origin frame. World remains responsible for account identity, purchases, multiple rooms, pet metadata and `profiles.room` serialization. The frame receives a display projection and reports selection, movement, furniture taps and walking. It never writes to local storage or Supabase. Only the existing **확인** action commits a furniture draft; **취소**, account changes and stale-room guards preserve committed data.

Legacy `wall: R`/pixel heights and avatar palette indices are converted for display without rewriting saved data. All 479 existing furniture/pet variants map to 3D models. Purchased wallpaper/floor artwork is projected from the existing catalog. Album, diary, guestbook and pet taps use the existing World actions and permissions.

The standalone demo's local save/import/reset, duplicate menus, synthetic friend, public-place simulation and retired claw machine are excluded. Three.js 0.160.0 and its RoundedBoxGeometry helper are vendored with their license, so this renderer has no runtime CDN dependency. Render failure or WebGL context loss reveals the existing SVG editor. Geometry and materials are released on replacement/unmount; offscreen rendering pauses.

Validation: `npm --prefix tests test`. `world-room-3d-ui.test.cjs` runs without live account access and checks touch placement, confirm/cancel, autosave isolation, legacy data, room/account changes, furniture links, six screen sizes, every catalog model and the WebGL fallback.

`characters.js` shares the room's avatar/pet factories with the dressing room and pet care/chat views. `world-characters.js` mounts those views and caches visible profile portraits. It never changes ownership, purchases, pet stats or chat storage. Avatar selections and pet care updates retain their WebGL frame. The 85 existing avatar choices remain distinct, including legacy hairstyles, clothing, shoes and accessories. Sleeve offsets use their actual length rather than RoundedBoxGeometry's unit-box parameters.

Settled rooms and character previews stop drawing; explicit pet reactions and walking still animate. Room shadows update when scene geometry moves, and animated furniture parts are found once at model construction. Profile portraits render during idle time, are cached, and share an available renderer. The house has separate interior/exterior materials and pitched lavender eaves. The left door, external yard, fence geometry and stairs are removed. The home camera fits only the house shell; a background illustration fills the surrounding corners without changing room coordinates or dimensions. `world-characters-ui.test.cjs` checks model parity, arm placement, paid previews, all avatar choices, pet actions/chat, renderer reuse, idle drawing, responsive layouts and fallback.


`places.js` and `world-places.js` render the existing café, library, park and arcade state. Furniture IDs, people, presence, seat targets, chat, quiz and game actions still come from World. Raycast hits invoke those existing actions; the iframe never creates replacement game or social data. Static model geometry is batched by material; a settled scene stops drawing and covered/offscreen scenes pause. Chair directions follow their layout and public shelves face inward.

`pet-breeds.js` provides different silhouettes, ears, muzzles, coats and tails for the 24 named dog/cat breeds, plus the two base pets. All surfaces use the same factories. Full hats suppress incompatible high hair pieces; accessory positions and portrait framing preserve their visibility.

`furniture.js` repairs solid openings, coplanar window art and hidden interiors in tubs, basins, sinks, fountains, plant leaves, bread racks and vending machines. `variants.js` preserves the catalog's species, rug/mat shape, pattern and illustration hints. The item list renders visible 3D thumbnails on demand, caches them, and shares a renderer. Missing colors use the model's own default, rather than universal brown. Saved item IDs, colors, room dimensions and ownership remain intact.

The park retains `assets/yard-lawn.webp` (72 KB) as its ground texture. The home uses `assets/room-backdrop.webp` (119 KB), generated with the built-in image generator and converted to WebP without changing its composition. It is a CSS background on `html[data-view="room"] body`, behind the transparent WebGL canvas, with no geometry, hit targets or animated rendering cost. Avatar, pet, portrait and public-place views keep their own backgrounds.

Backdrop prompt: "A soft pastel miniature 3D-style garden backdrop for a large separately rendered cutaway house. Calm flat sage lawn through the center, continuous scenery to every edge. No house, room, roof, island, raised platform, stairs, water, people, text or UI. Gentle daylight and dusty lavender shadows."

`world-places-ui.test.cjs` covers all four live-state adapters, furniture previews, game/table taps, profiles, walking, chairs, chat, detached-frame isolation, idle drawing, responsive layouts and WebGL fallback. Catalog visual review covered 256 base furniture types and the model variants; the existing 479-item construction gate is retained.


## Authored character and pets (native v20 assets)

The avatar screen offers **포근한 산책**, the authored v20 short-haired sweatshirt/pants/sneaker character, and **내 옷장**, the existing 85-choice wardrobe. The new style supports skin, hair, top and bottom colors. It is selected explicitly and stored as `avatar.renderStyle`; selecting it also records the current shape fields in `avatar.sculptedWardrobe`. Existing shape selections and purchased items are retained. A temporary shop try-on renders the requested garment without saving it. Equipping another hairstyle, garment, face, shoe or accessory exits the authored outfit and restores wardrobe rendering, rather than hiding the equipped choice.

`sculpted-avatar.js` adapts the shared 13-bone GLB to the room, character preview, care scenes, portraits and public-place renderer. Walking uses the rig; seated knees bend; lying down closes the eyes. Style and color changes retain the preview iframe/renderer. Public-place static batching preserves skinned hierarchies, and character views pause when covered, offscreen or hidden. Clone geometry, materials, bones and GPU skeleton data are independent and released on disposal.

`sculpted-pets.js` uses the authored, vertex-painted, 17-bone dog and cat GLBs. The base dog and corgi (`dog`, `dg_corgi`) share the dog asset; the base cat and mackerel tabby (`cat`, `ct_mackerel`) share the cat asset. Other species and breeds, including the fox factory's older cat-derived base, retain their previous silhouettes. Selected coats preserve cream markings and tabby bands. Existing room items, pet names, care stats, placement, ownership and actions still come from World.

The three models use locally vendored Three.js r160 GLTFLoader/SkeletonUtils. Optional preloads run concurrently and have a 5.5-second deadline; a missing, invalid or stalled model falls back to the existing renderer. Model sizes and SHA-256 checksums are in `assets/models/provenance.json`. This adds initial asset loading and skinning work; it is not a measured mobile FPS improvement. The native client itself remains a separate offline prototype.

No background, marketplace, database schema, account privileges, prices or purchase entitlements change in this integration. Tests `world-sculpted-avatar-ui.test.cjs` and `world-sculpted-pets-ui.test.cjs` cover model loading, rig/color isolation, style persistence projection, wardrobe/shop preview behavior, posing, clone disposal and asset fallback. The existing room, 85-choice character and public-place suites remain regression gates.
