# Marble rolling on wood

`wood-ball-roll-clean.wav` and `wood-ball-impact-clean.wav` are edited from a real recording
by **KaleidacousticsAudio**: “Marbles_Rolling on wooden floor 2.wav”.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/KaleidacousticsAudio/sounds/629002/
- Recording: single marbles rolling on a wooden floor and hitting the wall,
  recorded in X/Y stereo with a Zoom H5.
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Source preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/629/629002_13875907-hq.mp3
- Downloaded: 2026-10-30.

Both files are mono 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV, with DC removal, an 80 Hz
high-pass and 3.8 kHz low-pass. No synthesized noise or ceramic-bowl audio
is included. The previous ceramic recordings have been removed.

The rolling loop uses source seconds 29.50–32.20 with an 80 ms cosine wrap
crossfade, producing a 2.62-second loop (RMS 0.077, peak 0.336).
The contact uses seconds 15.020–15.210, with 2 ms attack and 20 ms release
fades, an additional 3.2 kHz low-pass and a damped tail (peak 0.600).

Conservative, fixed spectral cleanup uses the source's quiet 0.10–0.30-second
lead-in as a noise reference, with attenuation capped at 7 dB. There is no
time-varying gate. The original wood body is retained: 150–1,200 Hz energy
changes by only -0.35 dB, while rumble and rough upper bands fall by roughly
2.6–6.4 dB. The rolling waveform correlation with the previous edit is 0.9978.

The game derives contact speed from the trajectory relative to the stationary
bowl or spinning rotor. Playback speed, brightness and volume follow that
speed with smooth transitions. Stationary and airborne balls have no rolling
sound. Chip and win accents remain separate.
